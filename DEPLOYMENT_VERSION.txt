- Automatic rotating-partner schedules for Singles Americano.
- Automatic full round-robin schedules for Team Americano.
- Automatic round and court assignments based on available courts.
- Public Americano score entry with no sign-in.
- Live player or team Americano standings.
- Americano participant and court match filters.
- Americano results in Active, Upcoming, and History pages.
- Admin tools to clear test scores while keeping the schedule.
- A one-time Supabase migration that preserves all existing regular tournament records.

- Court options are generated automatically from scheduled matches.

- Group schedules display in match creation order, including team-filtered results.

- Losing teams show a crying emoji and red Loser label.

- Upcoming opens the nearest upcoming tournament directly.
- Upcoming uses the Active layout with score entry hidden.
- Upcoming, Active, History, Predict, Teams, Players and Records appear in the bottom navigation.
- Admin appears as a shield icon in the top header.
- Mobile leaderboard rows stay at one consistent height.
