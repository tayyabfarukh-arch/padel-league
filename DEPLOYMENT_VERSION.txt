Created: September 19, 2026

- Registration fees, payments, expenses, and financial totals are displayed in Kuwaiti Dinars (KWD).

- Duplicate-player protection across team registrations in the same tournament.
- Public registered-team list with registration and payment-progress badges.
- Admin registration settings, approvals, waitlist controls, payment records, and private notes.
- Confirmed registrations are added to the tournament participant list automatically.
- Tournament expense tracking and live expected, received, outstanding, expense, and balance totals.
- SUPABASE_REVAMP_V2_REGISTRATION_UPDATE.sql preserves all existing records.
- Profile claims now use a searchable visual player picker with profile pictures and names.
- Approved player accounts now display their complete linked career statistics and partner history.
- Player account creation with username, private email, and password.
- Safe claims that link accounts to existing player profiles after Admin approval.
- Self-service player name and profile-picture updates.
